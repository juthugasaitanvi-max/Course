My animal fact file

Snow leopards

Type of animal

Snow leopards are solitary and primarily active during dawn and dusk.

Where they live

Snow leopards inhabit the mountain ranges of Central Asia, including the Himalayas.

What they eat

Snow leopards are carnivores and primarily hunt prey such as ibex, blue sheep, and marmots.

A cool fact about snow leopards is that they can run up to 40 miles per hour almost faster than a car.
